    Optional features#

    Everything here is off by default, or switches itself on when the resource it needs is running. Defaults and the full setting list are in Configuration; the admin commands, logs and exports are in Server tools.

    Coming soon

    Rows marked soon are built and tested outside the game, but aren't in a released version yet.

    Loot and rewards#

    Feature What it does Setting
    Grabbing A full trolley pays over TOB.GrabTime seconds; X stops early and keeps what's in the bag TOB.TrolleyCash, TOB.StopGrabKey, TOB.LootCounter
    Gold and diamond trolleys One trolley can hold gold (2×) or diamonds (3×). A missing model falls back to cash TOB.SpecialTrolleyChance, TOB.SpecialTrolleys
    Deposit boxes 8 boxes per vault to drill while it's open, once each, with a drill item. Cash, items or nothing, by weight TOB.DepositBoxes, TOB.DrillRewards, TOB.DrillItem
    Loot as items Pay an item instead of cash. Full pockets never lose loot: players are told to make room TOB.RewardItem, TOB.RewardItemCount
    Marked bills One bag per trolley with its value on the item ({worth = ...}), for QBCore and Qbox laundering. Needs qb-inventory or ox_inventory; ESX pays cash TOB.MarkedBills

    Heist rules#

    Feature What it does Setting
    Crew size Robbers must stand near the panel to start. Without OneSync every online player counts TOB.MinCrew, TOB.CrewRadius
    Stopping bank chains One heist at a time on the server, and a cooldown on every bank after any heist TOB.OneAtATime, TOB.GlobalCooldown
    Restart protection Blocks new heists before a txAdmin restart, so nobody loses one halfway. Skipping the restart allows them again SV.BlockBeforeRestart
    Vault item The robber opens the vault by hand with thermite or a drill, staying near the bank. The item is used up TOB.VaultItem, TOB.VaultItemTime
    Crew takeover If the leader disconnects, the nearest crew member leads the rest of the heist —
    Why a heist can't start (soon) Every refusal says why, with the time left. "Not enough police" hides the numbers unless you show them SV.ShowPoliceCount

    Police and the chase#

    Feature What it does Setting
    Dispatch ps-dispatch works out of the box; any other dispatch script through a hook TOB.Dispatch, TOB.BuiltInPoliceAlert
    GPS tracker Some loot hides a tracker: police follow the robber on the map for a few minutes. Needs OneSync TOB.Tracker
    Dye pack Some trolleys burst into red smoke when the grab ends, ruining part of the money TOB.DyePack
    Using another dispatch script

    Paste the alert call from its documentation into one of these. An error in your code doesn't stop the heist; it shows in the console.

    In config/config.lua, runs in the robber's game:

    TOB.DispatchAlert = function(coords, bank)
    -- your dispatch script's alert, using coords
end

    In config/config_server.lua, runs on the server:

    SV.DispatchAlert = function(bank, coords, playerId, label)
    -- your dispatch script's server-side alert
end

    With ps-dispatch, add camId = 1 to a bank in config/banks.lua to attach a camera.

    Look and feel#

    Feature What it does Setting
    Hacking minigame The minigame before the hack, and the one for the boxes: an ox_lib skill check, none, a tobs_minigames game, or your own function TOB.HackMinigame, TOB.DrillMinigame, TOB.CardTime
    Minigames from tobs_minigames (soon) GTA Online's hacking laptop and drilling screen, GTA V's safe dial and nine more ensure tobs_minigames
    Minigames per bank (soon) Each bank can use its own; Paleto comes with GTA's laptop and drill minigames in config/banks.lua
    Animations and alarm The laptop hack, thermite sparks, the real Paleto alarm, and (soon) everyone nearby hearing the drill and the vault TOB.LaptopHack, TOB.VaultItemAnim, TOB.Alarm, SV.BankSoundRange
    Translations English, Danish, German, Swedish, Norwegian and Dutch, all in locales/locales.lua TOB.Locale
    Your own minigame, and the tobs_minigames games

    Any function works as a minigame, as long as it returns true when the player passed:

    TOB.HackMinigame = function(bank)
    return exports["my_minigame"]:Start(...) -- see that resource's documentation
end

    With tobs_minigames running (ensure it above tobs_bankrobbery), a setting can also name one of its games: "hack", "drill", "safe", "thermite", "keypad", "wires", "lockpick", "fingerprint", "hotwire", "lasers", "keyfiling" or "tracker". The GTA drill replaces the progress bar, since it can't be finished faster than TOB.DrillTime. Without tobs_minigames, or if a GTA screen doesn't load, the normal minigame runs instead.

    Per bank, in config/banks.lua:

    B1 = {
    label = "Paleto Bay (Blaine County Savings)",
    minigames = {hack = "hack", drill = {type = "thermite", difficulty = "hard"}},
    ...

    To add a language, copy the en block in locales/locales.lua, rename it, translate it and keep the %s and %d placeholders.

    Your own banks#

    The banks live in config/banks.lua: Paleto (B1) and the six Fleeca banks (F1–F6). Turn one off with enabled = false, all Fleeca banks with TOB.FleecaBanks = false, and give any bank its own cash and cooldown.

    Adding a bank

    Copy a bank's block, rename it (for example B2) and fill in the positions. Stand on each spot in game and type /bankcoords: it prints your position and heading ready to paste.

    Setting What it is
    doors.startloc The "Start bank heist" panel. animcoords is where the player stands
    gate The security gate: position, closed angle (h) and prompt position (txtloc)
    vault The vault door and its prompt position
    prop.first Where the used card is left
    trolley1–trolley3 The 3 trolley spawns, with angle (h)
    objects The same 3 spots, used for cleanup
    doors.secondloc Optional second panel that opens the gate, like Fleeca
    label The bank's name in Discord logs
    minigames (soon) This bank's own hack and drill minigame

    Banks with other door models can set gateModel and vaultModel (a model name or a hash number).
