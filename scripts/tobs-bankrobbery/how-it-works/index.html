    How it works#

    The heist#

    1. At least TOB.mincops police must be online (on QBCore and Qbox: on duty), and at least TOB.MinCrew robbers must be at the panel.
    2. A player with a Malicious Access Card uses Start bank heist at the bank's security panel (press E, or with ox_target).
    3. The card is used up and police are alerted. At Paleto, the bank alarm starts ringing.
    4. The robber opens a laptop at the panel and plays the hacking minigame: an ox_lib skill check, or GTA Online's hacking laptop at banks that use tobs_minigames (Paleto by default). Failing it, or taking longer than TOB.CardTime, ends the heist.
    5. The hack runs for TOB.hacktime (1 minute by default).
    6. If a vault item is set, the robber has TOB.timer seconds to plant thermite on the vault door, which then burns through.
    7. The vault opens. The crew has TOB.timer (5 minutes) to loot the 3 trolleys, with a countdown at the bank. Grabbing pays for the time spent, and X stops early. At Fleeca banks the third trolley is behind an inner gate that has to be hacked from a second panel. See Grabbing, gold and diamond trolleys and the deposit boxes.
    8. When every trolley is empty or the time runs out, the vault closes after TOB.VaultCloseDelay seconds. The bank goes on cooldown (TOB.cooldown, 10 minutes). Everyone sees what they got, and the crew's total.

    The heist also ends if the robber leaves the bank. If the robber disconnects, the nearest crew member at the bank takes over and leads the rest of the heist. Heists can also be blocked by the global rules, restart protection or an admin's heist switch; the player is always told why.

    For police#

    • Every officer gets an alert, and a flashing red blip marks the bank until the heist ends. Officers who go on duty during a heist get the blip too.
    • Officers can lock and unlock the security gate and the vault door.
    • Police can't start or loot the heist.
    • Using a dispatch script? See Dispatch.

    Anti-cheat#

    The server runs the whole heist: every step, every timer and every payout. Players' games only ask, so mod menus can't skip steps or fake payouts:

    • The vault only opens when the server says the hack (and the vault item) is done. Nobody can open it early.
    • Trolleys can only be looted while the vault is open, and the Fleeca gate trolley only after the gate is hacked.
    • Trolleys pay for the time actually spent grabbing, never more than the trolley holds, and only at the trolley.
    • Each trolley can be looted once per heist, and a player drills one deposit box at a time, for as long as drilling takes.
    • Only police can lock and unlock the doors. The robber's gate and vault are opened by the server.
    • Starting, looting and drilling check that the player is there. This needs OneSync; without it the console shows a warning.
    • A heist that somehow gets stuck ends automatically, and cooldowns survive restarts.

    Blocked attempts can be posted to Discord.
