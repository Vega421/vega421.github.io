    tobs_chat#

    v1.0.0 Qbox 2 languages Cfx escrow

    A chat made for roleplay. Every message is a small glass card, and the channel reads like a radio call: LSPD │ SERGEANT Sofie Rask ID 7. Out-of-character chat, /me and /do with speech bubbles, police and EMS radio, a staff channel and announcements, with the moderation tools staff need.

    Buy · $15 Installation

    At a glance#
    Framework Qbox (QBCore support is coming in an update)
    Requires ox_lib
    Replaces The default chat resource. Every script written for it keeps working
    Performance 0.00 ms idle, also with the chat open; 0.01 ms when a message arrives
    Languages English, Danish
    Licence Paid, delivered through Cfx asset escrow. config.lua, the texts, the framework bridge and the web files are open: What you can edit

    Features#

    • Channels and radios

      /me, /do, /ooc, police and EMS radio and a staff channel, each with its own colour, range, job and rank. Radios get their own tab.

      Channels

    • Speech bubbles

      /me and /do also show above the player's head, follow them while they walk, and hide behind walls.

      Features

    • Moderation

      Mutes that survive a reconnect, slow mode, a chat lock, spy mode, word and spam filters, announcements and Discord logs.

      Moderation

    • Five themes and a settings menu

      Every player picks a theme, accent colour, text size, width and position in their own menu.

      Themes and settings

    Support#

    Questions or a bug? Ask on Discord: support for the script is free. Please say your Qbox version and what the F8 and server consoles show.
